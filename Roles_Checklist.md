# MC Checklist for Code People meetings
## One Week Before Code-People Meeting
### General
- [ ] Verify room is still available in google calendar
### When Final Agenda is Received from Talk Coordinator
- [ ] Update code-people.github.io 
- [ ] Send announcement to Code People email list (based on TBD template).   
- [ ] Send announcement on Code People Slack channel.  

## Day of the Code-People Meeting
### Before the Meeting:
- [ ] Confirm all presenters are present!  
- [ ] Talk to presenters to clarify how you will signal them about time.   
- [ ] Set up lunch poll in Slack channel.  
- [ ] Write the confirmed lightning talks on the whiteboard.

### During the Meeting
- [ ] Thank folks for donuts (Foundation, Kirk Madson) and coffee (FundIT).  
- [ ] Announce next meeting/non-meeting.  
- [ ] Call for lightning talks.   
- [ ] Ask for future talk ideas (can just be requests, committee will try to figure out who can give the talk). 

# Talk Coordinator Checklist for Code People meetings
## After Committee Meeting
- [ ] Reach out to the contacts on the agenda to confirm the topic and who is giving the talk.
- [ ] When confirmed, update the [agenda](https://github.umn.edu/code-people/meetings/blob/master/agendas.md) with the talk presenter names, time slot, and to say `(confirmed)`.
- [ ] If talk slots open up, work to fill them by reaching out to other folks who have suggested talks in the [issues](https://github.umn.edu/code-people/meetings/issues). 

## Tuesday Prior to Code-People Meeting
- [ ] Send the final meeting agenda to the MC

# New Issue Coordinator Checklist
- [ ] Respond to new [issues](https://github.umn.edu/code-people/meetings/issues) as they come in
