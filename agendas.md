- November AICOP ShareTime: AI Gateway, Wednesday, November 18, 2026, 12:00 Zoom
The AI Community of Practice November ShareTime features a preview of the pending UMN AI Gateway. We will start with a short presentation from AI Gateway Team Member Bradley Kauffman, who will discuss how:

“The AI Gateway provides a supported, secure, one-stop point for the University community to access leading AI models. The AI Gateway Virtual Team will share a brief status update and demonstration of how we’re evolving the platform from a working pilot into a sustainable University service through improved onboarding, support, documentation, monitoring, cost visibility, governance, and operational readiness."

After the presentation, AICOP hopes to facilitate a conversation amongst our expert and novice guests in which we can build our capacity. We will surface questions through questions like: 

What type of work and projects can take advantage of an AI gateway?

Who else should know about the AI Gateway?

What organizations can assist with planning?

What skills do we need to develop to make use of these new models?

This meeting will be recorded and shared with the AICOP Community.
